How to play

Connect before they do

Classic

Take turns dropping a disc into a column. It falls to the lowest free space. Connect the chosen number horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to win.

Chaos mode

A flip or rotation uses your turn, changes the board’s orientation, and then reapplies gravity. Rotating a rectangular board swaps its dimensions.

Ties

If a transformation creates lines for both players, the player who transformed loses. A full board or the same position occurring three times is a draw.

Controls

Click or tap a column, or use the arrow keys and Enter. Undo returns to the previous human decision in an AI game.
